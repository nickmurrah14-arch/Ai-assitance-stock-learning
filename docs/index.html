How it works

1. A call goes unanswered

Your phone forwards unanswered calls to your assistant line. You keep your number, and nothing changes for callers.

2. They get a text within seconds

"Sorry we missed your call! What can we help with?" The assistant gets their name, address, the problem, and when they're free. It only answers questions using facts you've approved.

3. You get the lead

A text with everything you need to call back and quote. Emergencies like a gas smell, flooding, or no AC for someone at risk get flagged to you right away.

Questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. We turn on "forward when unanswered" on your existing line. Calls you pick up work exactly like today.

Will it say something wrong to my customers?

It only uses the facts you give us (services, hours, area, emergency policy). It never quotes prices or promises arrival times. It tells customers your team will confirm those on the call.

What about emergencies?

If someone mentions a gas smell, carbon monoxide, sparking or smoke, it tells them to get out and call 911 or the gas company, and alerts you immediately. Flooding and no AC for someone at risk also get flagged urgent.

Does it pretend to be a person?

No. It says it's your automated assistant, and customers can text STOP at any time.

What do I need to do?

About 20 minutes with us to go over your services and hours, and one call-forwarding setting on your phone. We handle the rest, including registering your business for texting with the carriers, which usually takes a few days to two weeks.
